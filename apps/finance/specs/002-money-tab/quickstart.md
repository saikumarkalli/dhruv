and the feature's own source. Entities: [data-model.md](data-model.md). Routes:

  working. `NxTextField` error state, `NxButton` sizes/loading, `SelectionSheet`, `NxSelect` exist
  in `:libs:core` (Phase 2 deliverables that D3/D5 consume).
- This phase's migration applied to `dhruv-dev` — six tables, three views, the audit trigger,
  `merge_categories`, and the extended `delete_my_data()` (see `data-model.md`). Authored
  declaratively under `supabase/schemas/finance/`, migration generated by
  `supabase db diff -f money_phase3`, grants hand-added (ADR-0032).
- `:apps:finance:feature:money` registered in `settings.gradle.kts` (with its `projectDir` remap to
  `apps/finance/feature/money/money/`) and wired into `CalculatorApplication`'s Koin graph.
- `money` flag present in `platform/feature-flags/dhruv-finance.json`.

## Coverage validation

```powershell
# Merged report + floor check (both are part of regressionCheck above; run alone to iterate)
./gradlew jacocoAggregatedReport jacocoCoverageVerification

# Human-readable per-module numbers (same parser CI uses for the Job Summary / PR comment)
python scripts/ci/regression_summary.py
```

Read the HTML at `build/reports/jacoco/jacocoAggregatedReport/jacocoAggregatedReport.html`; the XML
beside it is what the parser and the floor check consume.

Three things to confirm, in order:

1. **The module is measured at all.** `:apps:finance:feature:money` appears as its own row in the
   summary. If it shows up under `(other)`, `_FEATURES` in `scripts/ci/regression_summary.py` is
   missing `"money"`; if it does not appear anywhere and its classes are absent from the HTML
   report, `coveredModules` in the root `build.gradle.kts` is missing the module path. Either way
   the gate is passing vacuously for this phase's code.
2. **Merged line coverage rose**, and `globalLineFloor` is ratcheted to just under the new measured
   figure — never above it (Article X / ADR-0013: a non-regression ratchet, not a target). If
   coverage did not rise, the floor stays put and the checkpoint note says why.
3. **Both numbers are recorded**, not one. A feature module's own percentage is dragged down by
   Compose screen files the JVM gate never executes; the meaningful figure for this phase is
   coverage of the repository/ViewModel logic in `:apps:finance:data`'s `tracker/repo` and the
   module's ViewModels. Record the module total and the logic total side by side in the QA
   catalog's §14 summary — reporting one and implying the other is how a coverage number stops
   meaning anything.

Expected: all green. Every test cites an `MNY-*` scenario ID from
`apps/finance/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-qa-test-scenario-catalog.md` §4 — a test with no
citation is a review-blocking finding (constitution Article I).

After the migration lands, regenerate the web types so the parallel web track stays typed:

```powershell
supabase gen types typescript --schema public,finance > web/src/shared/types/database.ts
```

## Manual end-to-end scenarios (mirror spec.md's Acceptance Scenarios)

1. **Story 1 — three-tap quick add** (`MNY-UI-001`, `MNY-FLOW-001`)
   Money tab → FAB → type an amount → accept the guessed category → accept the guessed account →
   Save, counting taps after the amount.
   **Expect**: ≤3 taps; the row appears under today's group; the pinned month summary changes by
   exactly that amount.

2. **Story 1 — transfers are not spend** (`MNY-BR-001`)
   Record a TRANSFER between two of your own accounts.
   **Expect**: both accounts' balances move; the month's EXPENSE total and every category share are
   unchanged. Verify against `v_month_summary` directly, not only on screen — the exclusion must
   hold at the view, since Phase 4/5 read the same view.

3. **Story 2 — read the month** (`MNY-UI-002`, `MNY-UI-003`)
   With transactions across several days, open the ledger; change the month selector; open the
   filter sheet and toggle a category.
   **Expect**: day grouping with per-day net; summary and list both follow the month selector; the
   result count updates *before* "Show N results" is tapped, and matches the list after applying.
   Save the filter as a view and re-apply it.

4. **Story 3 — spendable now** (`MNY-BR-002`, `MNY-UI-004`)
   Create a bank, a cash and a credit-card account; put spend on the card.
   **Expect**: `SPENDABLE NOW` = bank + cash only; the card shows a negative balance under the
   "owed, not held" group with limit, utilisation and due date.

5. **Story 3 — reconciliation** (`MNY-UI-005`)
   Back-date an account's `reconciled_at` beyond 30 days; open D6 and D7; reconcile with a balance
   that differs from the computed one.
   **Expect**: staleness flag on both screens; after reconciling, the flag clears and the difference
   appears as a visible adjustment entry (never a silently changed opening balance).

6. **Story 4 — audit trail** (`MNY-BR-006`, `MNY-UI-006`, `MNY-FLOW-004/005`)
   Create a transaction, change its category, open its detail; then use Duplicate, then "Make
   recurring".
   **Expect**: history lists creation and the category change in order with old → new; Duplicate
   opens an unsaved pre-filled entry (nothing written until saved); "Make recurring" opens the form
   with the recurring toggle pre-filled. The budget-impact line is **absent this phase** — deferred
   to Phase 4 by design, not a bug.

7. **Story 5 — categories** (`MNY-BR-003`, `MNY-BR-004`, `MNY-UI-007`)
   Rename a category with transactions; then merge two categories holding N and M transactions.
   **Expect**: rename changes only the label, transactions still linked; the merge dialog states
   "N+M transactions will move" and that it cannot be undone; after confirming, the surviving
   category holds N+M and nothing is orphaned. Kill the app mid-merge if you can — the count must be
   all-or-nothing (it is one SQL function, research R9).

8. **Story 6 — recurring never posts silently** (`MNY-BR-005`, `MNY-FLOW-002`, `MNY-UI-008`)
   Create a recurring entry due today from D3's "make it recurring"; reopen the app twice.
   **Expect**: no ledger row is written; exactly **one** pending entry exists after two opens (the
   `(recurring_id, due_on)` idempotency key); D9 shows the review banner, monthly in/out totals, the
   next-30-days list with auto-debit vs variable tags, and the paused section after pausing one.
   Accepting writes the transaction with an "from recurring" history entry; dismissing writes
   nothing.

9. **Home's deferred row** (FR-034)
   With a credit-card account carrying a `due_day` in the near future, open Home.
   **Expect**: UPCOMING lists both the loan/EMI row (Phase 2) and the card-bill row (this phase);
   tapping the card row opens that account's detail.

10. **State contract** (FR-032, NFR-4)
    Sign out and open Money; sign back in, enable airplane mode, force-kill and reopen with nothing
    cached; open a month with no transactions.
    **Expect**: `SignedOutCard`, then `OfflineStateCard`, then an empty state with a verb CTA — on
    every one of D1–D9, never a blank screen or an unresolving spinner.

11. **Consent gate** (NFR-1, Article VIII)
    Settings → Privacy → turn "Sync my financial records" off, then open Money.
    **Expect**: zero PostgREST requests leave the device (verify on a proxy or via the interceptor's
    own test), and every Money surface degrades to its consent-off state.

12. **Scale** (SC-009)
    Seed ~5,000 transactions in one month on the dev project.
    **Expect**: D1 opens and scrolls without visible stutter; the month summary and category shares
    come from the views, not from a client-side sum (check that no request pulls the full month's
    rows purely to total them).

## Definition of done for this quickstart

Every numbered scenario passes manually, `regressionCheck` is green, the three coverage checks above
pass (module measured by name, floor ratcheted to the measured value, both numbers recorded), and
every `MNY-*` row in the QA catalog is closed (✅) or explicitly deferred with a stated reason — constitution Development Workflow step 7 (checkpoint gate). The Sec step
additionally verifies, against the dev Supabase project, the three rules this phase deliberately put
in the database: the audit trigger fires on every mutation path, `transaction_events` has no
UPDATE/DELETE policy, and RLS isolates all six new tables per user.
